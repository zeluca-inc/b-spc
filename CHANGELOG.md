# Changelog

## v2.6 — 1 October 2026
Supersedes v2.3. The version number now follows the AGBER methodology the standard accompanies; no v2.4 or v2.5 of the standard was issued, and references to "B-SPC v2.4" elsewhere mean v2.3 read with AGBER v2.4.

**Control discipline (from AGBER v2.4)**
- Limit setting (Phase I) and monitoring (Phase II): "limits provisional" until 20–25 stable subgroups; limits re-set after a process change, re-baseline or regime window (§5).
- Default Nelson detection set 1, 2, 5 and 6 with published in-control run length; rules 3, 4, 7 and 8 as investigation aids (§5).
- g-chart / time-between-escapes CUSUM for rare-event nodes (§5).
- Named process owner, standard work and out-of-control action plan for every controlled node (§5).
- Numeric scale gate agreed before the trial; permutation or wild-cluster inference below ten assignment units (§7).

**Targets, projections and indicators (from AGBER v2.5)**
- PROJECTED tag: not an evidence grade, never trended beside a measured rate (§3).
- Target and projection rules: R* = threshold − z·SE; product-form lever projection; Feasible / Stretch / not feasible (§11.1).
- Faster designs: in-tool assignment, lottery-ordered waves, group-sequential boundaries, bandits, uplift routing, acceptance sampling (§7).
- Indicator selection (six criteria), case-level validation (RR, attributable dollar share, AUC, lead time), audit samples, quarterly re-validation and retirement; bounded role for language models (§4).
- Four legitimacy rules for a lower reported rate (§10); measurement independence from whoever delivers a countermeasure (§12).

**Two bills, attribution and capacity (from AGBER v2.6)**
- The two bills: benefit cost-share tiers from FY2028 and the 75% state share of administrative costs from FY2027 (§11.2); two-bills strip (§5).
- Attribution in error dollars: attribution status for every initiative, dollar chain, overlap credited once, rate bridge with the unattributed remainder always shown (§7); attribution statement at each FNS release, budget request and CAP date (§11.3).
- Scale-readiness card: error effect, capacity ρ ≤ 0.85, yield to beat, guardrails; Scale / Scale with conditions / Hold (§8).
- Capacity and process-efficiency measures (touch time, cases per worker, process cycle efficiency, failure demand, first-pass yield, new required work, household outcomes, released capacity) and the order in which released hours are spent (§8).
- Figure statuses LOCKED / UNAVAILABLE / REFUSED; vendor-reported figures as activity only; the year-and-source rule (§3).
- Seven new defined terms (§1); conformance rows 14–21 and §14.1, the applicability of every row at the Look (Phase 0); worked example extended (§13).
- US spelling throughout.
